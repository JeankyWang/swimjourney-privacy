# Privacy Policy for SwimJourney



Operator: swim joinery  
Contact: wzqzch@icloud.com



## Information you enter
SwimJourney lets parents or coaches enter swimmer profiles and swimming records, including names, birth dates, training and competition results, notes, and optional injury or allergy information. You may also add photos or videos. Please obtain the appropriate guardian's permission before entering or sharing a child's information.



## Storage and use
In the current version, profiles, records, photos and videos are stored on your device and used to provide the app's features. The app does not upload those records to the operator's servers. Depending on your device settings, Apple device backups may include app data. Deleting the app or data from the device may not delete copies in device backups, exported files or files you have shared.



## Photos, camera and notifications
The app accesses selected photos or files, and uses the camera and microphone when you choose to record video. Screenshot text recognition runs on the device; the selected screenshot is not uploaded by the app. If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules notifications on your device. You can manage permissions in device settings.



## Exports and backups
You can export or share reports, records, templates and backups. These files may contain personal or health information. App-created backup files are not encrypted; protect them and review recipients before sharing. Third-party services and recipients may retain their own copies.



## Purchases
Subscriptions are processed by Apple using StoreKit. The app checks subscription transactions to provide access; child profiles and records are not sent for purchasing. Apple handles payment information under its own policies.



## Children
SwimJourney is intended for use by parents or coaches managing swimming information. If a child's data is entered, the adult using the app is responsible for obtaining the necessary guardian authorization. Contact us with questions about a child's information.



## Your choices and contact
You can edit or delete records in the app, manage device permissions, and contact swim joinery at wzqzch@icloud.com with privacy questions. Data in separately saved or shared copies must be managed separately. We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change; the current version will be available on this page.
